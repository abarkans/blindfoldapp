"How does this actually work" is a fair question to ask before you hand over your evening to an app. Not the pitch version — the actual mechanics: what you type in, what BlindfoldDate does with it, and what you're allowed to change afterward.

---

## What you set up once

Setup takes longer to describe than to do — under thirty seconds, start to finish. You tell it your taste — the categories you're into, from food and nature to art, nightlife, or fitness — your rough budget, and whether tonight is a night out or a night in. That's the entire input. There's no long quiz, no calendar sync, nothing else to fill in.

That one setup is what everything downstream runs on. You're not filling this in per date; you set your taste once and adjust it later if it stops fitting.

---

## Your first date runs at full range

Before any of the settings below kick in, there's one thing that happens automatically: your very first date after registering is a full-range pick — every category open, the widest radius — regardless of which plan you end up on. It's less a "trial" you have to remember to cancel and more a first taste of the widest version of the app before you settle into a tier. One thing to know: using it starts the clock. Your next free date unlocks 30 days after that first one, counted from the day you use it rather than the calendar month.

---

## What you can adjust after that

From your second date on, a settings screen opens up where you set your own search radius, switch which city you want venues searched in, and adjust your category preferences — this is real, editable control, not something fixed by the app on your behalf. What's capped is how far those settings can go, and that's tied to plan.

Free lets you set a radius up to 15km and choose from 3 categories. Plus — the paid plan — extends that to 50km and unlocks every category. Either way, the number is a ceiling you set within, not a hidden default — you can set a Free account lower if you want something closer, you just can't push it past 15.

City works the same way: if you're travelling, or planning around a trip, you can switch which city BlindfoldDate is searching for venues in rather than waiting for it to catch up with your device.

---

## The reveal

Whoever signs up sends an invitation to their partner; the partner accepts, and the two accounts are linked. From that point, you both see the same date revealed at the same moment — nobody gets a preview, nobody has to pretend to be surprised. That simultaneous reveal is the actual mechanism behind the "mystery" in the name — it's not that BlindfoldDate keeps it secret from you specifically; it keeps it equally unknown to both of you until the same moment. It's the same logic behind [why a mystery date works](/blog/mystery-date-ideas-for-couples) in general — the not-knowing has to be shared to do anything.

Each date comes with the practical numbers up front once it's revealed — expected duration and a budget range, the same way you'd see "3 hrs, $20–30" on a listing — so you're not walking in blind on logistics, only on the venue itself. And every date includes a small challenge attached to the venue, built to give the evening a bit of a spine instead of just showing up and hoping for conversation.

---

## If the pick isn't right

You're not locked into whatever comes up. There's a swap option if the first suggestion misses — wrong vibe, wrong night, whatever the reason. Free accounts get one swap, ever, so it's meant for a genuine miss rather than a way to browse. Plus accounts get a swap on every date, which matters more once you're using it regularly rather than once a month.

---

## Free vs. Plus, plainly

Free gets you one planned date a month, pulled from 3 of the available categories, a 15km radius ceiling, one swap total, and the XP and badge system still tracks your progress. Every date's photo gets saved too — you just can't see the gallery yet.

Plus unlocks all twelve categories instead of three, extends your radius ceiling to 50km, lets you set your own date frequency instead of one every 30 days, gives you a swap on every date instead of one ever, doubles the XP you earn, opens up the more creative, less obvious picks that don't make the free rotation, and unlocks your memories gallery — including everything you've already saved while on Free. It's $0.99 for the first month, then $2.99 a month after that, and you can cancel it yourself from account settings — no support ticket required.

Neither plan asks for a card to start, and Free stays free indefinitely. The $0.99 first month applies whenever you upgrade — there's no signup window to miss, so waiting a few months on Free doesn't cost you the intro price.

## What builds up over time

A few things accumulate that don't affect whether tonight happens, but change whether you keep going. Completed dates earn XP and unlock badges — mostly there so your fifth date feels different from your first. Each date also has a photo moment, and those photos become your memories gallery: every plan saves them, only Plus shows them, and upgrading later unlocks everything you've already banked rather than starting you from zero. And there's a quiet nudge if a few weeks go by without a date, which exists for the same reason the app does — the plan you meant to make is easy to lose to a busy week if nothing reminds you.

Everything above runs from the website — registration, setup, and the reveal all happen there — with a native Android app available if you'd rather have it on your homescreen. There's no iOS app; on iPhone, the website is the whole thing.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I choose the exact venue, or set a search radius?

You can't pick the specific venue — that's the mystery part — but you can set your own radius from your second date onward, up to 15km on Free or 50km on Plus. Your very first date runs at full 50km range regardless of plan.

### Can I change my city if I'm travelling?

Yes, from the same settings screen that controls radius and categories. You don't have to wait for it to catch up with your device — you can switch it manually ahead of a trip.

### Do I lose my date photos if I stay on Free?

No. Every photo gets saved regardless of plan — you just can't view the gallery until you're on Plus. If you upgrade six months in, everything from your Free months is there waiting, not lost.

### Does my partner need their own account?

Whoever signs up sends an invitation; your partner accepts it and you're linked. After that, you both see the same reveal at the same time.

### When do I get my next free date after the first one?

30 days after you use the first one — the countdown starts from that day, not from the start of the calendar month.

### What happens if we hate the suggestion?

Use your swap. Free gives you one, ever; Plus gives you one on every date. It's not unlimited browsing — it's there for a genuine miss, not a way to shop the list.

### Is the free plan actually usable, or just a trial?

It's a real, ongoing plan — one date a month, three categories, a 15km radius ceiling, no card required, no expiry. Plus is there if once a month starts feeling like not enough.

### Does BlindfoldDate work for long-distance couples?

The app doesn't — it's built around real venues near you, so it needs you both in the same place. The Long Distance Relationship Mission Pack in the store does: two PDFs, one per partner, designed to be opened together on a call.

### Do I need an account to buy the printable packs?

No. Pay once, download straight away, and they're yours to keep. Each pack has a free sample if you want to see the format before buying.
